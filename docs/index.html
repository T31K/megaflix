Install on your TV

  1. On the TV, go to Settings → System → About and click Android TV OS build a few times to enable Developer options (only needed once).
  2. In Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions, turn on Unknown sources for your browser / downloader app.
  3. Open this page in the TV browser (or a "Downloader" app) and press Download APK.
  4. Open the downloaded file and choose Install, then launch Megaflix from your apps.
